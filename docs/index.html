interactive drawn geometry for 703 cells across 11 libraries. drag a card to orbit; open it for layer controls, pin metadata, and an exploded view.

703 cells across 11 libraries; 703 rendered so far. Every card is live geometry — drag to orbit, open for layers and explode view.

Cells render as per-layer merged prisms extruded with the lyd25 z-stack — WebGL2 (shared context, depth-buffered, blitted per card; painter's-algorithm canvas fallback), no libraries. Geometry loads only while a card is near the viewport and is dropped when it scrolls away. Very large macros are level-of-detail capped (largest shapes kept) and say so in their viewer. The spin-video edition remains at index.html.
